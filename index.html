Selected Projects

Project 01 · Remote Sensing & GIS

Climate-Driven Lake Dynamics Analysis using Remote Sensing and ERA5 Data

Geospatial analysis of global lake responses to drought periods using satellite altimetry, ERA5 climate reanalysis data, DEM-based lake morphology and long-term hydrological time-series.

Study Area:
Lake Victoria, Lake Michigan, Lake Malawi and Lake Urmia across Africa, North America and Asia.

Data:
Satellite altimetry, HydroSat surface water extent, ERA5 precipitation / evapotranspiration, DEM data.

Methods:
ArcGIS, QGIS, remote sensing, spatial analysis, hydrological monitoring, time-series analysis.

Key Results:
Produced global lake maps, long-term water-level trends and volume-change analysis to compare lake responses during drought periods.

Project 02 · Climate Analytics & Hydrological Modeling

Hydrological Drought Analysis & Climate Time-Series Modeling

Statistical drought analysis focused on the relationship between lake-level anomalies, drought indices, precipitation, evapotranspiration and basin-scale climate dynamics.

Research Focus:
Hydrological drought detection, climate response modeling and lake-level anomaly interpretation.

Data:
ERA5 climate reanalysis, SPI, SPEI, PDSI drought indices, satellite-derived lake water levels.

Methods:
Python, weighted least squares adjustment, statistical trend estimation, multi-variable time-series analysis.

Key Results:
Identified drought phases, compared drought indicators and visualized climate-driven hydrological responses.
